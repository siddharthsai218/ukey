Whether your phone is in your pocket, across the room, or on your desk: uKey gives you three flexible biometric options to authenticate on Linux—and mirrors every single verification to your phone's tamper-proof audit log.

OPTION 01 • LAPTOP BUILT-IN SENSOR

Laptop Palm-Rest Fingerprint

If your ThinkPad, Dell, or Framework has a working fingerprint sensor, authenticate in 0.4s right on the keyboard palm-rest via pam_fprintd.so. Zero need to reach for your phone.

No fingerprint reader on your laptop? The built-in webcam (/dev/video0) verifies your face locally in 0.5s (Linux Hello). 100% offline processing; no images ever leave RAM.

The Mobile Security Audit Ledger: Zero-Cloud Privacy Guarantee

Every laptop login tracked in real-time on your phone

Whichever method you choose on your laptop (laptop fingerprint, webcam face scanner, or mobile biometric), uKey transmits a cryptographically signed verification receipt over the local USB/Wi-Fi socket directly to your phone app.

Why this is a breakthrough for privacy and security:
• Tamper-Proof Audit Trail: If an attacker sits at your laptop or a rogue script runs sudo, your phone immediately vibrates and logs the command out-of-band.
• Zero Biometrics Transferred: Your face scans and fingerprint templates never leave local silicon. Only the signed cryptographic assertion and timestamp are synced.
• 100% Local / Zero Cloud: No servers, no telemetry, no tracking. Everything runs point-to-point on your local machine.

Not a cloud passkey.

Not a browser extension.

A native hardware security key.

Google and Apple passkeys route through Bluetooth, require cloud rendezvous, and only work inside browser tabs. They cannot unlock your Linux terminal. They cannot authenticate sudo. They cannot sign an OpenSSH commit.

uKey bypasses all of that. By emulating a real USB Human Interface Device directly inside the Linux kernel on /dev/uhid, your desktop sees an actual plugged-in YubiKey—powered by the tamper-resistant hardware enclave already inside your pocket.

< 15 ms

Hardware cryptographic signing time inside Android's StrongBox Keymaster. From kernel event to authenticated return with zero cloud transit.

LAYER 01
Kernel /dev/uhid
Emulates a physical USB HID device (Vendor ID 0x1209, Product 0x0001) over BUS_USB.
LAYER 02
CTAPHID Framing
Handles 64-byte segmented packets, channel allocation (CID), and 100ms keep-alive pulses.
LAYER 03
CTAP2 State Machine
Strict CBOR encoding for authenticatorMakeCredential and authenticatorGetAssertion (ES256).
LAYER 04
Local TCP Bridge
Length-prefixed JSON wire protocol over USB (adb reverse) or local Wi-Fi. Zero external telemetry.
LAYER 05
Android StrongBox / TEE
Hardware-isolated NIST P-256 private keys protected by Android BiometricPrompt. Keys never leave silicon.

Get started in minutes

Run the installer on your Linux host, load the Android app, and touch your fingerprint.

# 1. Install uKey daemon and kernel rules
git clone https://github.com/siddharth/ukey.git && cd ukey
sudo ./setup_udev.sh
python3 -m venv .venv && .venv/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt

# 2. Start the daemon
.venv/bin/python daemon/ukey_daemon.py

# 3. Setup phone and connect
adb reverse tcp:8443 tcp:8443
adb install -r android/app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk
